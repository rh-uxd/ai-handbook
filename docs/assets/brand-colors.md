# Red Hat Brand Color Reference

This document defines the official Red Hat color palette for the **Red Hat Hybrid Illustration Style**—a fusion of IBM's geometric precision and Google's expressive warmth. Every illustration must follow these color constraints to maintain brand consistency and visual impact.

---

## Core Palette (Use 2-3 per illustration)

The foundation of every illustration. These colors provide structure, clarity, and the essential Red Hat brand identity.

| Color Name  | Hex Code  | Token/Reference | Usage                                    |
| ----------- | --------- | --------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Red Hat Red | `#EE0000` | red-50          | **MANDATORY** accent, always present     |
| Warm Black  | `#1A1A1A` | black           | Structure, grounding, primary shapes     |
| White       | `#FFFFFF` | —               | Space, clarity, light backgrounds        |
| Cool Gray   | `#6A6E73` | gray-50         | Supporting structure, secondary elements |

### Red Hat Red: The Non-Negotiable Accent

**Red Hat Red (#EE0000) is MANDATORY in every illustration.** It commands attention and anchors the composition. Use it as a focal accent—never flooded across the entire canvas. Maximum 30% of composition.

Red Hat Red should be **visually first**—the element that draws the eye immediately upon viewing the illustration.

---

## Secondary Palette (Pick ONE family per illustration)

Choose one secondary color family to support Red Hat Red. These colors add semantic meaning and conceptual variety while maintaining brand focus.

| Color Name  | Hex Code  | Token     | Theme                | When to Use                                        |
| ----------- | --------- | --------- | -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Teal        | `#009596` | teal-40   | Technology, data     | Technical diagrams, data flows, ML pipelines       |
| Blue        | `#0066CC` | blue-50   | Enterprise, trust    | Business concepts, security, reliability           |
| Purple      | `#7C3699` | purple-50 | Platform, innovation | AI features, advanced capabilities, transformation |
| Orange      | `#EC7A08` | orange-40 | Energy, community    | Collaboration, human-centered concepts, warmth     |
| Yellow/Gold | `#F4C145` | gold-40   | Warmth, creativity   | Ideation, generation, creative output              |
| Cyan        | `#009BDE` | cyan-40   | Cloud, openness      | Accessibility, transparency, sharing               |

**Usage rule:** Use secondary colors at 10-20% opacity for organic background blobs, or at full opacity for supporting objects. Never let secondary colors compete with Red Hat Red for visual prominence.

## People Palette (For FIGURATIVE & HYBRID illustrations only)

Use Red Hat's people palette exclusively—never stylize skin with brand or accent colors. Each person gets one skin tone family, chosen realistically.

### Cool Skin Tone Families (lean pink/rose)

| Tone Level  | Primary Shade | Highlight Shade | Usage                        |
| ----------- | ------------- | --------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Light Cool  | `#F5CFC0`     | `#E8A898`       | Fair complexions, cool tones |
| Medium Cool | `#C47F6A`     | `#A05C48`       | Medium complexions, rosy     |
| Deep Cool   | `#6B3428`     | `#4A2018`       | Deep complexions, cool tones |

### Warm Skin Tone Families (lean yellow/brown)

| Tone Level  | Primary Shade | Highlight Shade | Usage                          |
| ----------- | ------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Light Warm  | `#F6D5B0`     | `#E8B882`       | Fair complexions, golden tones |
| Medium Warm | `#C8874E`     | `#A06030`       | Medium complexions, warm       |
| Deep Warm   | `#7A4020`     | `#512810`       | Deep complexions, warm tones   |

**Usage notes:**

- Each person uses one skin tone family (not mixed)
- Hair, eyes, and features should match skin family in realistic tones
- Lip color subtle—no stylized cartoon mouths
- Clothing follows light source (upper left) for shading consistency

**Only apply this palette when illustration type is FIGURATIVE or HYBRID.**

## Color Usage Guidelines

### Illustration Type Color Strategies

**ABSTRACT Illustrations:**

- Red Hat Red + 1 secondary color + core neutrals
- Organic background blob in secondary color at 10-15% opacity
- Bold geometric color blocking with intentional negative space

**OBJECT-BASED Illustrations:**

- Red Hat Red on primary object (the hero element)
- Secondary color for supporting objects or environment
- Warm Black/Cool Gray for structural elements and connectors

**FIGURATIVE Illustrations:**

- People use realistic skin tones from People Palette
- Red Hat Red on key object person interacts with
- Secondary color for environment/background elements
- Clothing in neutrals or subtle secondary color

**HYBRID Illustrations:**

- Combine people (realistic skin tones) with geometric elements
- Red Hat Red on the technology/system element being engaged with
- Secondary color for abstract environment shapes

### Gradient Usage

**Permitted:** Subtle gradients on organic background blobs and background shapes only.

**Forbidden:** Gradients on primary subject matter, people, or key geometric elements. Never invent gradients outside Red Hat's defined gradient pairs.

### Background Selection

**White (#FFFFFF):** Use for approachable, navigational, educational content. Creates clean, open feeling.

**Warm Black (#1A1A1A):** Use for technical, foundational, platform concepts. Creates sophisticated, serious tone.

**Rule:** Select based on content hierarchy and user intent, not arbitrary aesthetic preference.

## Composition & Color Balance

### The 1-2-3 Rule

Every illustration uses:

1. **ONE mandatory accent:** Red Hat Red (MANDATORY, visually first)
2. **TWO-THREE core colors:** From Core Palette
3. **THREE supporting elements:** Max of secondary color + neutrals

**Never exceed this constraint.** More colors = visual chaos.

### Red Hat Red Placement Strategy

Red Hat Red should be placed using the **visual first principle:**

- Center of composition (draws eye naturally)
- Upper third of canvas (eye tracks top-to-bottom)
- Contrasted against neutral background (maximum pop)
- On the primary narrative element (the "hero" of the illustration)

### Negative Space

White space is as intentional as color. 40-50% of composition should be negative space (background color or organic blob shapes). Never cluttered.

## Accessibility Requirements

### Contrast Compliance

All illustrations must meet WCAG AA standards:

- Text labels: Minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio
- Large text/shapes: Minimum 3:1 contrast ratio

**Test with:** WebAIM Contrast Checker or browser DevTools

### Color Independence

Never rely on color alone for meaning:

- Use shapes, patterns, and labels
- Directional indicators (arrows, lines)
- Size/position hierarchy

**Particularly critical:** Blue/Orange and Green/Red combinations are challenging for colorblind users. Add texture, pattern, or label when using these together.

## Implementation Checklist

When creating illustrations, follow this order:

- [ ] 1. Choose illustration type (ABSTRACT, OBJECT-BASED, FIGURATIVE, HYBRID)
- [ ] 2. Select background: White or Warm Black
- [ ] 3. Add organic blob in secondary color (10-15% opacity)
- [ ] 4. Build geometric composition using Warm Black and Cool Gray
- [ ] 5. **Add Red Hat Red to hero element (MANDATORY, visually first)**
- [ ] 6. Apply secondary color to supporting elements (max 20% composition)
- [ ] 7. Add people (if FIGURATIVE/HYBRID) using People Palette
- [ ] 8. Apply shadows from upper-left light source
- [ ] 9. Verify Red Hat Red is visually first
- [ ] 10. Test contrast ratios for accessibility
- [ ] 11. Verify total color count ≤ 6 unique colors

## Examples

### ABSTRACT Illustration Color Recipe

- Background: White
- Organic blob: Cyan #009BDE at 12% opacity
- Primary shapes: Warm Black, Cool Gray
- Accent: Red Hat Red on central geometric form
- Result: 4 colors total (White, Cyan, Warm Black + Cool Gray + Red Hat Red)

### OBJECT-BASED Illustration Color Recipe

- Background: Warm Black
- Primary object: Red Hat Red (laptop, container, gear)
- Supporting objects: Cool Gray
- Connectors: White lines at 60% opacity
- Result: 4 colors total (Warm Black, Red Hat Red, Cool Gray, White)

### HYBRID Illustration Color Recipe

- Background: White
- Organic blob: Blue #0066CC at 15% opacity
- Person: Medium warm skin tone #C8874E
- Technology element person interacts with: Red Hat Red
- Environment shapes: Warm Black, Cool Gray
- Result: 6 colors total (max complexity)

## Migration from Old Palette

**Previous illustrations used:**

- Iconoir icons with 1.5px strokes
- Multiple secondary colors per composition
- Dark backgrounds (#151515) and light backgrounds (#F0F0F0)

**New Red Hat Hybrid Style requires:**

- Geometric shapes (no pre-made icons)
- Limited color palette (Red Hat Red + 1 secondary + core neutrals)
- Warm Black (#1A1A1A) replaces old dark (#151515)
- Organic background blobs replace flat backgrounds
- Mandatory Red Hat Red in every composition

**All 80 illustrations are being redesigned to match this updated palette.**
