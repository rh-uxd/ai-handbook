## Overview

Governors standards ensure humans remain in control of AI systems by providing oversight mechanisms, approval gates, and transparency into AI actions. Unlike fully autonomous systems, governed AI operates as a collaborative partner—making recommendations and assisting with tasks while preserving human authority over important decisions.

These standards recognize that AI should augment human judgment, not replace it. By building in checkpoints, audit trails, and override capabilities, Governors standards create AI systems that are accountable, transparent, and ultimately controllable by the humans they serve.

**These standards implement Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) principles** — ensuring that humans maintain authority over AI-assisted decisions, with clear accountability and oversight at appropriate risk levels.

---
## Key Principles
1. **Human authority** - Users maintain final decision-making power
2. **Transparency** - AI actions are visible and traceable
3. **Reversibility** - Ability to undo or modify AI decisions
4. **Accountability** - Clear record of who approved what
5. **Trust through control** - Agency builds confidence in AI
---
## When to use these standards
- **High-stakes decisions** - Production system changes, financial transactions, data modifications, and customer-facing actions
- **Regulated environments** - Healthcare, finance, government contracts, and other compliance-sensitive operations
- **Multi-user collaboration** - Team workflows, shared assistants, and organization-wide tools that need attribution
- **Learning and improvement** - Understanding AI behavior, debugging unexpected outcomes, and performance optimization
