## Overview

Identifiers & Branding standards establish how users recognize AI involvement in their workflows. As AI becomes deeply integrated into products, users need clear visual and textual signals that distinguish AI-generated content from human-created work, AI-powered features from traditional functionality, and AI processing states from system operations.

These standards go beyond simple labeling—they create a cohesive visual language that builds brand recognition, sets appropriate expectations, and enables transparency. When users see consistent AI identifiers across products, they develop mental models of what AI can do and how to interact with it effectively.

---
## Key Principles
1. **Consistency breeds recognition** - Same visual treatment across all AI features builds familiarity
2. **Clarity over cleverness** - Obvious identifiers beat subtle hints every time
3. **Transparency builds trust** - Clear labeling of AI involvement prevents confusion and establishes accountability
4. **Brand coherence** - AI visual language extends core brand, doesn't contradict it
5. **Accessibility first** - Never rely on color or icons alone; always provide text alternatives
---
## When to use these standards
- **AI-generated content** - Text, code, configurations, recommendations, documents, or media produced by AI
- **AI-powered features** - Conversational interfaces, suggestions, automated analysis, and predictive capabilities
- **AI product branding** - Dedicated AI product lines, feature sets, and communications about AI capabilities

## Related Categories
- **Trust builders**: Clear identification enables transparency and accountability
- **Governors**: Visual markers help users distinguish AI actions requiring oversight
- **Conversation**: Chat interfaces use visual language to distinguish AI from human
- **Generation and output**: Generated content needs clear AI identification
