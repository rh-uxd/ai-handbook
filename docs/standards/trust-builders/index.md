## Overview

Trust Builders are standards that help users understand how AI systems work, make decisions, and maintain reliability. In an era where AI is increasingly integrated into critical workflows, users need to understand not just what AI does, but why and how—and know when to verify or override AI decisions.

These standards address fundamental questions: "Can I trust this output?", "How did the AI arrive at this conclusion?", "What are the limitations?", and "What happens if something goes wrong?". Trust is earned through transparency, consistency, and giving users appropriate control.

---
## Key Principles
1. **Explain reasoning** - Show how the AI arrived at outputs or decisions
2. **Acknowledge limitations** - Be transparent about what the AI cannot do
3. **Provide confidence indicators** - Help users assess reliability of outputs
4. **Enable verification** - Make it easy to check and validate AI outputs
5. **Maintain consistency** - Build trust through predictable behavior
6. **Show provenance** - Reveal sources and data used in AI reasoning
---
## When to use these standards
- **High-stakes decisions** where accuracy is critical
- **Regulated industries** requiring auditability and transparency
- **Professional tools** where users stake their reputation on outputs
- **Sensitive content** involving personal or confidential information
- **New AI features** where users are still building mental models
- **Unexpected results** that might surprise or concern users
---
## Related Categories
- **Error handling**: When things go wrong, transparent error standards maintain trust
- **Governors**: Controls that prevent harmful outputs build confidence
- **Generation and output**: How outputs are presented affects perceived trustworthiness
- **Identifiers and branding**: Clear labeling of AI-generated content supports transparency
