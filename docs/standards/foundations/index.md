## Overview

Foundations are the ethics and responsibility standards that apply no matter which product, modality, or UI pattern you are designing. They cover bias and fairness, human oversight, transparency and information integrity, sustainability, and related obligations that should shape every AI experience.

Use these standards alongside the functional categories in this library. A composer, alert, or approval flow still needs to be fair, overridable, and honest about what the model did.

---
## Key Principles
1. **Apply across the product** - Ethics is not a separate flow; it shapes every AI surface
2. **Fair and inclusive outcomes** - People get usable, comparable results across contexts and abilities
3. **Humans stay in control** - Users can understand, refuse, override, or correct AI actions
4. **Honest about limits** - Coverage gaps, uncertainty, and model behavior are visible at the point of use
5. **Sustainable by default** - Prefer the least expensive option that still helps, and keep non-AI paths complete
---
## When to use these standards
- **Any AI feature** shipping in a Red Hat product, regardless of category or modality
- **High-stakes or uneven quality** where bias, oversight, or transparency is part of the design problem
- **Cross-cutting reviews** when checking that a pattern also meets ethical obligations
---
## Related Categories
- **Governors**: Human oversight, approval, and audit of AI actions
- **Trust builders**: Transparency, provenance, and confidence in outputs
- **Accessibility**: Inclusive, operable AI-specific UI
- **Error handling**: Failures, alerts, and recovery when AI goes wrong
